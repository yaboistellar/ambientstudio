WHY WE SYNTHESIZE EVERYTHING

Most gaming studios record pre-baked MP3 files. At Ambient Studio, we believe code-based procedural synthesis is faster, infinitely funnier, and 1000x lighter on your bandwidth.

🦆

Mathematical Duck Formants

Human and animal vocal tracts produce distinct formant bands. By routing an aggressive 320Hz sawtooth oscillator into an FM modulator running at 36Hz and filtering at 950Hz Q4.2, we recreate duck quacks mathematically.

⚡

Zero Buffer Delay (0ms Latency)

Pre-rendered audio files require network requests, decoding buffers, and memory caching. Web Audio API synthesizers fire instantaneously on the hardware audio thread with zero latency and zero stutter.

🪶

Sub-Kilobyte Footprint

A standard 10-second MP3 clip weighs 200KB+. Our entire interactive soundboard containing 7 distinct retro instruments and background chiptune drones weighs less than 4KB of pure JavaScript.
